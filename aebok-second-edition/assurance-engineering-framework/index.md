## Why an Assurance Engineering Framework?

Scientific disciplines explain reality.

Engineering disciplines transform reality.

The Assurance Engineering Framework™ provides a structured engineering process for applying Assurance Engineering principles to real operational environments.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Engineering Framework™ is the integrated engineering methodology for designing, evaluating, operating, improving, and governing trustworthy operational environments.

---

# Core Principle

Trustworthy operation is achieved through a continuous engineering cycle rather than isolated governance activities.
