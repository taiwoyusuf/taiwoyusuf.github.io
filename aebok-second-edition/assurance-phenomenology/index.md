## Why Assurance Phenomenology?

Every engineering discipline studies observable phenomena.

Assurance Engineering studies assurance phenomena.

These phenomena represent the observable manifestations of Operational Trust within complex operational environments.

Understanding assurance phenomena enables organizations to observe, classify, explain, predict, and improve Operational Trust.

---

# Definition

Assurance Phenomenology™ is the systematic study, classification, interpretation, and prediction of observable assurance phenomena.

---

# Core Principle

Operational Trust itself cannot always be observed directly.

Instead, Assurance Engineering observes assurance phenomena that indicate the current condition of Operational Trust.
