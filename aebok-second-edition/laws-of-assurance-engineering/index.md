# Proposed Laws of Assurance Engineering™

# Chapter 10

---

## Why Laws?

Engineering disciplines are built upon fundamental statements that remain true across applications.

These proposed laws describe how Operational Trust behaves regardless of industry, technology, or regulation.

Unlike implementation guidance, these laws are intended to remain stable as technologies evolve.

---

# Law 1

## Trust Requires Evidence

Operational Trust cannot exist without evidence.

Claims do not create trust.

Evidence creates trust.

---

# Law 2

## Trust Changes Whenever Evidence Changes

Operational Trust is dynamic.

Every new piece of evidence has the potential to increase, decrease, or maintain Operational Trust.

Trust is never permanently fixed.

---

# Law 3

## Trust Naturally Decays

Without refreshed evidence, Operational Trust decreases over time.

Time itself is an assurance variable.

---

# Law 4

## Every Assurance Object Has a Trust State

Every regulated object possesses a continuously changing assurance state.

Whether measured or not, the trust state always exists.

Assurance Engineering seeks to measure it.

---

# Law 5

## Every Trust Decision Must Be Explainable

Operational Trust without explanation is merely opinion.

Every trust computation must be traceable back to its supporting evidence.

---

# Law 6

## Human Accountability Cannot Be Delegated

Artificial Intelligence may assist.

Humans remain accountable for regulated decisions.

Responsibility cannot be transferred to software.

---

# Law 7

## Assurance Exists Within Context

Trust is contextual.

An Assurance Object may be trustworthy for one intended use while being unsuitable for another.

Operational Trust is never absolute.

---

# Law 8

## Better Evidence Produces Better Trust Decisions

The quality of Operational Trust is constrained by the quality of assurance evidence.

Incomplete evidence limits trustworthy decision-making.

---

# Law 9

## Assurance Is Continuous

Operational Trust should be continuously maintained rather than periodically demonstrated.

Inspection readiness should become a continuous operational capability.

---

# Law 10

## Assurance Must Improve the System

The purpose of Assurance Engineering is not documentation.

Its purpose is improving the trustworthiness, safety, resilience, and effectiveness of operational systems.

---

# Relationship to the Assurance Stack™

These proposed laws govern every layer of the Assurance Stack™, from Evidence Sources™ through Governance Vision™.

---

# Relationship to Platform A

Every Platform A product should demonstrate compliance with these proposed laws.

---

# Relationship to Platform B

Platform B explores, validates, extends, and mathematically formalizes these proposed laws through research and industrial implementation.

---

# Future Evolution

As Assurance Engineering matures through industrial application, peer-reviewed research, and independent validation, these proposed laws may be refined, expanded, or reformulated while preserving the discipline's central objective:

To continuously establish, measure, explain, and improve Operational Trust.
