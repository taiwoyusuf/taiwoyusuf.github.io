# Assurance Observation Theory™

# Chapter 61

---

## Why Assurance Observation Theory?

Every assurance conclusion originates from one or more observations.

Evidence is derived from observations.

Knowledge is derived from evidence.

Operational Trust™ is derived from knowledge.

Understanding Assurance Engineering therefore begins with understanding the nature of Assurance Observations™.

---

# Definition

An Assurance Observation™ is a structured observation describing an assurance-relevant property of an Assurance Object™, Assurance Relationship™, Assurance Ecosystem™, or Assurance State™.

---

# Core Principle

No assurance conclusion exists without one or more observations.

Observations are the fundamental building blocks of Assurance Engineering.

---

# Sources of Assurance Observations

Observations may originate from:

- Human inspection
- Sensors
- AI analysis
- Validation activities
- Environmental monitoring
- Cybersecurity monitoring
- Operational events
- Regulatory inspections

---

# Observation Characteristics

Every Assurance Observation™ should describe:

- What was observed
- When it was observed
- Where it was observed
- How it was observed
- Confidence
- Context
- Source
- Traceability
