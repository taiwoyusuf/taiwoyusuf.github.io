# Future Foundations™

## Emerging Research Directions for Assurance Engineering™

The concepts on this page represent active areas of research that have not yet been incorporated into the formal Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK™).

They are maintained here to guide future research, industrial validation, doctoral work, publications, and future editions of AEBOK™.

As evidence accumulates through implementation and peer-reviewed research, selected concepts may mature into core chapters of the discipline.

---

## Current Research Incubator

### Assurance Operating System™ (AOS™)

A future orchestration layer capable of coordinating Assurance Twins™, Operational Trust™, Assurance Graphs™, AI agents, and human decision-making into a unified assurance runtime.

Status:
Concept under development.

---

### Assurance Time™

The study of how Operational Trust evolves over time, including trust velocity, trust decay, assurance latency, recovery dynamics, and predictive assurance.

Status:
Research concept.

---

### Assurance Cognition™

The science of augmenting human assurance reasoning through evidence, AI, and contextual decision support.

Status:
Research concept.

---

### Assurance Reality™

A persistent assurance layer through which physical and digital objects continuously expose their operational assurance state to humans and intelligent systems.

Status:
Research concept.

---

### Trust Propagation Calculus™

A mathematical framework describing how Operational Trust propagates through Assurance Graphs™.

Status:
Future mathematical research.

---

### Collective Assurance Intelligence™

The study of how groups of humans and AI systems collaboratively develop, maintain, and improve Operational Trust.

Status:
Research concept.

---

### Autonomous Regulatory Reasoning™

The development of AI systems capable of reasoning about regulatory evidence while maintaining human accountability.

Status:
Research concept.

---

## Living Research Roadmap

These concepts will evolve through:

- PhD research
- Industrial implementation
- Platform B development
- Peer-reviewed publications
- Regulatory engagement
- Community collaboration

Future editions of AEBOK™ may promote selected concepts into the formal body of knowledge as they mature.
