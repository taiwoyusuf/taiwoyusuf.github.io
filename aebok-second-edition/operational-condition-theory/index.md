# Why Operational Condition Theory?

Every engineering discipline defines the primary phenomenon it seeks to understand.

Assurance Engineering™ studies the operational condition of complex socio-technical systems and how that condition can be observed, interpreted, communicated, and improved.

Operational Condition Theory™ establishes this central scientific focus.

---

# Definition

An Operational Condition™ is the current and evolving state of a socio-technical system as determined through structured observations, evidence, context, and engineering interpretation.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering decisions should be based on an accurate understanding of operational condition rather than isolated observations or assumptions.

---

# Characteristics of Operational Conditions

Operational conditions are:

- Dynamic
- Context-dependent
- Observable
- Interpretable
- Explainable
- Measurable
- Evolvable

---

# Sources of Operational Conditions

Operational conditions emerge from:

- Physical systems
- Human activity
- AI systems
- Environmental factors
- Organizational processes
- Regulatory context
- Digital infrastructure
