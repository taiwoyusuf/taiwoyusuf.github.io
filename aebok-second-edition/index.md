# Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK™)

## Second Edition

**The Scientific Foundation of Assurance Engineering™**

---

## Purpose

The Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK™) defines the principles, concepts, architectures, models, and methods used to continuously design, measure, validate, govern, and improve Operational Trust across regulated physical, digital, AI-enabled, and cyber-physical systems.

Unlike traditional governance frameworks that primarily demonstrate compliance, Assurance Engineering continuously answers one operational question:

> **Can this object be trusted right now, and what evidence proves it?**
