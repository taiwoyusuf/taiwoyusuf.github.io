# Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK™)

## Second Edition

**Work in Progress**

This Second Edition expands the original Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge by introducing:

- Continuous Assurance™
- Assurance Objects™
- Assurance Twins™
- Operational Trust™
- Governance Vision™
- Platform A
- Platform B
- Wearable Assurance
- Agentic Assurance
- Spatial Governance

This edition documents the evolution of Assurance Engineering into a complete scientific discipline for designing, measuring, validating, governing, and continuously assuring trust in regulated physical, digital, AI-enabled, and cyber-physical systems.
