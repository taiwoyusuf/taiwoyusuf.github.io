# Governance Vision™

---

## 7.1 Introduction

Governance Vision™ is the human interface of Assurance Engineering.

It enables engineers, operators, auditors, quality professionals, regulators, and decision-makers to visualize Operational Trust in real time using wearable devices, augmented reality, mobile applications, dashboards, voice assistants, and future spatial computing technologies.

Governance Vision™ brings assurance directly to the point of work.

---

## 7.2 Definition

Governance Vision™ is a human-centered assurance interface that transforms Operational Trust into understandable, explainable, and actionable information.

---

## 7.3 Purpose

Governance Vision™ allows users to answer questions immediately, including:

- Can this system be trusted?
- Is this equipment validated?
- Is this AI model approved?
- Is this batch inspection-ready?
- Are backups current?
- Has cybersecurity drift occurred?
- Are there open CAPAs?
- Is this object audit ready?

---

## 7.4 Supported Interfaces

Governance Vision™ supports:

- Smart Glasses
- Mobile Devices
- Tablets
- Dashboards
- Voice Assistants
- Augmented Reality
- Virtual Reality
- Mixed Reality
- Spatial Computing

---

## 7.5 Information Displayed

Governance Vision™ may display:

- Operational Trust Score™
- Assurance Twin™
- Validation Status
- Change History
- Risk Level
- Cybersecurity Status
- Backup Status
- CAPA Status
- Inspection Readiness
- Human Accountability
- AI Assurance Status

---

## 7.6 Relationship to Assurance Twins™

Governance Vision™ visualizes Assurance Twins™.

The Assurance Twin computes trust.

Governance Vision™ communicates trust.

An engineer wearing smart glasses should be able to look at any regulated object and immediately understand its assurance state without searching multiple systems.

Governance Vision™ aims to make Operational Trust visible wherever work occurs.
