# Foundations of Assurance Engineering™

---

## 1.1 Introduction

Assurance Engineering™ is proposed as a new interdisciplinary engineering discipline focused on continuously designing, measuring, validating, governing, and improving Operational Trust across regulated physical, digital, AI-enabled, and cyber-physical systems.

Traditional engineering disciplines primarily focus on designing systems that function.

Assurance Engineering focuses on continuously proving that those systems remain trustworthy throughout their operational lifecycle.

Its central question is:

> **Can this object be trusted right now, and what evidence proves it?**

---

# 1.2 Why Assurance Engineering?

Modern regulated industries are changing rapidly.

Artificial Intelligence.

Autonomous Agents.

Digital Twins.

Intelligent Manufacturing.

Radiopharmaceutical Production.

Clinical Trial Automation.

Cyber-Physical Systems.

These technologies create enormous opportunities while simultaneously increasing operational complexity.

Existing disciplines such as:

* Quality Engineering
* Systems Engineering
* Validation
* Computer System Assurance
* AI Governance
* Cybersecurity
* Risk Management

address parts of this challenge.

None provides a unified discipline dedicated to continuously measuring and maintaining Operational Trust.

Assurance Engineering addresses that gap.

---

# 1.3 Scope

Assurance Engineering applies to any regulated Assurance Object including:

* Computerized Systems
* Manufacturing Equipment
* AI Models
* AI Agents
* Manufacturing Operations
* Clinical Trial Processes
* Suppliers
* Facilities
* Digital Infrastructure
* Data Pipelines
* Batch Records
* Human Decisions

---

# 1.4 Vision

The long-term vision of Assurance Engineering is to establish Operational Trust as a measurable engineering property that can be continuously monitored, explained, predicted, and improved through evidence.

Rather than asking whether an organization is compliant during an inspection, Assurance Engineering seeks to enable organizations to continuously demonstrate inspection readiness before problems occur.

---

# 1.5 Relationship to AEBOK™

The Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK™) serves as the formal knowledge repository for the discipline.

It defines:

* Principles
* Terminology
* Models
* Architectures
* Methods
* Reference Implementations
* Research Directions

that collectively establish Assurance Engineering as an emerging engineering discipline.
