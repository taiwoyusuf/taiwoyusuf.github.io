## Why Assurance Design Science?

Engineering disciplines do more than analyze existing systems.

They create new artifacts, methods, models, architectures, and technologies that solve previously unsolved problems.

Assurance Engineering™ advances through purposeful design, rigorous evaluation, and continuous refinement.

---

# Definition

Assurance Design Science™ is the systematic creation, evaluation, and improvement of assurance artifacts intended to improve trustworthy operation.

---

# Core Principle

Scientific understanding explains the world.

Design Science improves it.

# Evaluation Criteria

Every assurance artifact should be evaluated for:

- Effectiveness
- Explainability
- Human usability
- Scientific validity
- Operational usefulness
- Scalability
- Reproducibility
- Ethical acceptability
