---

## Why Assurance State Theory?

Complex operational environments continuously change.

Equipment changes.

People change.

Evidence changes.

AI changes.

Cybersecurity changes.

Operational Trust therefore emerges from the evolving Assurance State of the environment.

---

# Definition

An Assurance State™ represents the complete observable condition of an assurance ecosystem at a specific point in time.

It integrates operational, technical, organizational, human, and regulatory dimensions into one coherent engineering representation.

---

# Core Principle

Operational Trust is not directly engineered.

The Assurance State is engineered.

Operational Trust emerges from it.
