# Chapter 3

# Assurance Objects™

---

## 3.1 Introduction

Assurance Engineering begins with the concept of the Assurance Object™.

An Assurance Object is any physical, digital, AI-enabled, or human-controlled entity whose Operational Trust must be continuously measured, governed, and improved.

Every assurance activity begins with identifying the Assurance Object.

---

## 3.2 Definition

An Assurance Object™ is any entity that requires continuous assurance evidence to demonstrate Operational Trust throughout its lifecycle.

---

## 3.3 Categories of Assurance Objects

Examples include:

* Computerized Systems
* Manufacturing Equipment
* AI Models
* AI Agents
* Clinical Trial Systems
* Clinical Signals
* Manufacturing Processes
* Batch Records
* Facilities
* Laboratories
* Data Pipelines
* Digital Twins
* Suppliers
* Personnel
* Decisions

---

## 3.4 Core Characteristics

Every Assurance Object possesses:

* Identity
* Ownership
* Lifecycle
* Evidence
* Risk
* Controls
* Accountability
* Operational Trust
* Assurance Twin

---

## 3.5 Assurance Object Lifecycle

Every Assurance Object progresses through:

1. Design
2. Build
3. Qualification
4. Validation
5. Operation
6. Monitoring
7. Change
8. Retirement

Operational Trust changes throughout this lifecycle.

---

## 3.6 Assurance Passports™

Every Assurance Object owns an Assurance Passport™ containing:

* Identity
* Owner
* Evidence
* Validation Status
* Cybersecurity Status
* Change History
* Risk Profile
* Trust Score
* Audit History

---

## 3.7 Relationship to Assurance Twins™

Each Assurance Object may be represented by one Assurance Twin™, providing a continuously updated representation of its assurance state.

---

## 3.8 Relationship to Platform A

Platform A manages Assurance Objects operationally through products such as:

* CITrust™
* ValidationTrust™
* AccessTrust™
* CyberTrust™
* Operational Trust™

---

## 3.9 Relationship to Platform B

Platform B extends Assurance Objects into advanced research including:

## 3.10 Research Direction

Future research includes:

* Object-to-object trust

* Autonomous Assurance Objects

* Intelligent Assurance Passports
