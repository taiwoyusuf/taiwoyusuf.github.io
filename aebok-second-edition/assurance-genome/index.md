# Chapter 13

## Why an Assurance Genome?

Operational Trust changes continuously.

However, every Assurance Object possesses intrinsic characteristics that remain relatively stable throughout its lifecycle.

These characteristics determine how assurance should be designed, interpreted, and governed.

The Assurance Genome™ captures these enduring characteristics.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Genome™ is the intrinsic assurance profile of an Assurance Object.

Unlike Operational Trust, which is dynamic, the Assurance Genome changes only when the fundamental identity or intended purpose of the object changes.

---

---

# Why It Matters

Two systems may both have an Operational Trust Score of 85%.

However, they should not necessarily be governed the same way.

One may support:

- sterile manufacturing

while another supports:

- cafeteria scheduling

Their Assurance Genomes are fundamentally different.

---

# Genome Stability

Operational Trust changes frequently.

The Assurance Genome changes rarely.

Examples of Genome-changing events include:

- New intended use
- Regulatory reclassification
- System redesign
- New patient safety role
- Major architectural transformation

---

# Relationship to Operational Trust™

The Assurance Genome determines how Operational Trust should be interpreted.

Operational Trust determines the current assurance condition.

The Assurance Genome determines the assurance context.
