# The Grand Problem Framework of Assurance Engineering™

# Chapter 69

---

## Why a Grand Problem Framework?

Engineering disciplines progress by solving problems of enduring scientific and societal importance.

The Grand Problem Framework of Assurance Engineering™ identifies the classes of problems that define the discipline and guide long-term research.

Rather than focusing on technologies, it focuses on problems whose solutions improve trustworthy operation across generations.

---

# Definition

The Grand Problem Framework™ classifies the enduring engineering problems that Assurance Engineering seeks to understand and solve.

---

# Core Principle

Technologies change.

Fundamental operational problems persist.

Assurance Engineering addresses the enduring problems rather than temporary technological implementations.

---

# Grand Problem Class 1

## Observability

How can invisible operational conditions become continuously observable?

Examples include:

- AI behavior
- Organizational readiness
- Evidence quality
- Human workload
- Emerging operational risk

---

# Grand Problem Class 2

## Explainability

How can complex operational conclusions be explained clearly enough for responsible human decision-making?

---

# Grand Problem Class 3

## Trustworthiness

How can Operational Trust™ be established, maintained, measured, challenged, and improved?

---

# Grand Problem Class 4

## Adaptation

How can assurance remain effective as technology, organizations, regulations, and operational environments evolve?

---

# Grand Problem Class 5

## Human Decision Support

How can Assurance Engineering improve human judgment without replacing human accountability?

---

# Grand Problem Class 6

## Scientific Measurement

How can assurance properties be measured consistently, reproducibly, and transparently?

---

# Grand Problem Class 7

## Continuous Learning

How can assurance ecosystems continuously improve through evidence, experimentation, and operational experience?
