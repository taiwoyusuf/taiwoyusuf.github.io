# Assurance Graph™

# Chapter 12

---

## Why an Assurance Graph?

Traditional governance evaluates systems individually.

Assurance Engineering recognizes that regulated environments consist of interconnected Assurance Objects whose trust relationships influence one another.

The Assurance Graph™ models these relationships.

---

# Definition

An Assurance Graph™ is a network representation of Assurance Objects, Assurance Twins, evidence sources, and trust relationships that enables Operational Trust to be analyzed across an entire enterprise.

---

# Nodes

Nodes represent Assurance Objects.

Examples include:

- Equipment
- AI Models
- AI Agents
- Manufacturing Systems
- Clinical Trial Systems
- Facilities
- Suppliers
- Personnel
- Validation Records
- Digital Services

Each node possesses its own Operational Trust.

---

# Edges

Edges represent assurance relationships.

Examples:

- depends on
- validates
- controls
- supplies
- monitors
- approves
- supervises
- owns
- authorizes
- inherits trust from

Edges themselves possess assurance properties.

---

# Trust Propagation

Operational Trust may propagate through the graph.

Examples:

- A failed backup server reduces trust in connected applications.
- An AI model with severe drift affects downstream clinical decisions.
- A supplier quality issue influences manufacturing readiness.
- A compromised identity reduces trust across connected systems.

Trust therefore behaves as a network property rather than an isolated score.

---

# Assurance Dependencies

The Assurance Graph identifies:

- Critical Dependencies
- Single Points of Assurance Failure
- Trust Bottlenecks
- Circular Dependencies
- Assurance Orphans
- Trust Islands

---

# Enterprise Assurance View

The Assurance Graph enables organizations to answer questions such as:

- Which Assurance Objects have the greatest influence on Operational Trust?
- Which failures create the largest trust impact?
- Which objects require immediate intervention?
- Which evidence improves trust most effectively?

---

# Relationship to Assurance Twins™

Every Assurance Twin occupies one node within the Assurance Graph.

The graph represents interactions among Assurance Twins rather than isolated objects.

---

# Relationship to Governance Vision™

Governance Vision™ enables users to visualize Assurance Graphs using dashboards, wearable devices, spatial computing, and augmented reality.

Engineers may explore trust relationships interactively rather than reviewing isolated reports.
