## Why a Scientific Method?

Engineering disciplines do not advance through terminology alone.

They advance through disciplined observation, experimentation, criticism, refinement, and reproducible evidence.

The Scientific Method of Assurance Engineering™ establishes the process through which new assurance concepts become accepted scientific knowledge.

---

# Definition

The Scientific Method of Assurance Engineering™ is the systematic process for discovering, validating, refining, and integrating new knowledge into the discipline.

---

# Core Principle

No concept becomes part of Assurance Engineering because it sounds useful.

Concepts become part of the discipline only after surviving scientific scrutiny.

# Acceptance Criteria

A proposed Assurance Engineering concept should demonstrate:

- A clearly defined problem
- Precise terminology
- Logical consistency
- Relationship to existing theories
- Potential for observation
- Potential for measurement
- Potential for experimentation
- Reproducibility
- Practical usefulness
- Openness to revision

---

# Scientific Evidence

Evidence supporting Assurance Engineering should include:

- Operational observations
- Controlled experiments
- Case studies
- Mathematical analysis
- Simulation
- Human factors studies
- Cross-domain validation
- Independent replication

---

# Role of Criticism

Scientific criticism strengthens the discipline.

Constructive disagreement is welcomed.

Evidence carries greater weight than authority.
