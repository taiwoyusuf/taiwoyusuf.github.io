# The Research Agenda of Assurance Engineering™

# Chapter 65

---

## Why a Research Agenda?

Scientific disciplines advance through coordinated research rather than isolated discoveries.

The Research Agenda of Assurance Engineering™ identifies the highest-priority scientific, engineering, mathematical, human, and technological questions that will shape the discipline over the coming decades.

---

# Vision

The objective of the research agenda is to establish Assurance Engineering™ as a scientifically rigorous engineering discipline through continuous experimentation, collaboration, and practical implementation.
