## Why a Canonical Model?

Engineering disciplines require a common conceptual model that organizes the discipline into a coherent and universally understandable structure.

The Canonical Assurance Model™ establishes the reference model through which every Assurance Engineering concept can be located, understood, and related to every other concept.

---

# Definition

The Canonical Assurance Model™ is the reference conceptual architecture describing how Assurance Objects™, observations, evidence, intelligence, Operational Trust™, and human decisions interact within an Assurance Ecosystem™.
