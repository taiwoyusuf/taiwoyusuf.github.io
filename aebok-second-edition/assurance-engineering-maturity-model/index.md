# Assurance Engineering Maturity Model™

# Chapter 67

---

## Why a Discipline Maturity Model?

Scientific disciplines mature over decades through research, education, experimentation, industrial adoption, and societal impact.

The Assurance Engineering Maturity Model™ provides a structured framework for evaluating the long-term evolution of the discipline itself.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Engineering Maturity Model™ describes the progressive stages through which Assurance Engineering evolves from an emerging concept into a mature global engineering discipline.

---

# Core Principle

Disciplines mature through evidence, education, implementation, and scientific contribution rather than popularity.

---

# Level 1 — Conceptual

Characteristics include:

- Initial terminology
- Foundational ideas
- Vision
- Early frameworks
- Individual researchers

---

# Level 2 — Scientific

Characteristics include:

- Peer-reviewed publications
- Formal definitions
- Experimental studies
- Mathematical models
- Independent critique

---

# Level 3 — Engineering

Characteristics include:

- Reference architectures
- Engineering methods
- Industrial pilots
- Operational implementations
- Design standards

---

# Level 4 — Educational

Characteristics include:

- University courses
- Textbooks
- Professional certifications
- Academic laboratories
- Graduate research

---

# Level 5 — Global Discipline

Characteristics include:

- International conferences
- Professional societies
- Standards organizations
- Journals
- Cross-industry adoption
- Continuous scientific evolution

---

# Indicators of Progress

Examples include:

- Number of universities teaching the discipline
- Number of independent research groups
- Number of validated experiments
- Industrial adoption
- International collaboration
- Standards development
- Educational resources
- Scientific influence
