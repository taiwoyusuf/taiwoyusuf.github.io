## Why Assurance Epistemology?

Operational Trust depends upon knowledge.

However, not all knowledge is equally reliable.

Organizations frequently make operational decisions using incomplete, uncertain, delayed, or conflicting evidence.

Assurance Epistemology™ studies the nature, quality, certainty, limitations, and justification of assurance knowledge.

---

# Definition

Assurance Epistemology™ is the study of how assurance knowledge is acquired, validated, justified, communicated, and used to support Operational Trust.

---

# Core Principle

Evidence is not knowledge.

Knowledge is not certainty.

Certainty is not trust.

These concepts must remain distinct.

# Assurance Certainty™

Every assurance conclusion possesses a certainty level.

Certainty depends upon:

- evidence quality
- evidence completeness
- evidence consistency
- source credibility
- timeliness
- explainability
- reproducibility

Operational Trust should always be accompanied by Assurance Certainty™.

---

# Uncertainty

Organizations must recognize:

- unknown evidence
- conflicting evidence
- incomplete evidence
- delayed evidence
- ambiguous evidence

Managing uncertainty is itself an assurance capability.

---

# Explainable Knowledge

Every assurance conclusion should answer:

How do we know this?

What evidence supports it?

How reliable is the evidence?

What assumptions remain?
