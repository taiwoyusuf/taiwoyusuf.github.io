# Assurance Operating System™ (AOS™)

# Chapter 16

---

## Why an Assurance Operating System?

Organizations operate hundreds of disconnected systems.

Each system answers only part of an assurance question.

The Assurance Operating System™ coordinates these systems into one continuous assurance environment.

Rather than replacing enterprise systems, the AOS™ orchestrates them.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Operating System™ is the orchestration layer of Assurance Engineering.

It continuously coordinates Assurance Twins™, Operational Trust™, Evidence Intelligence™, Human Decision-Making, AI Agents, and Governance Vision™ into one explainable assurance ecosystem.

---

# Core Principle

Traditional Operating Systems manage computing resources.

The Assurance Operating System™ manages assurance resources.

Examples include:

- Evidence
