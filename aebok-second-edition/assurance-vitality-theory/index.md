## Why Assurance Vitality?

Operational Trust measures the current assurance condition.

Healthy assurance ecosystems do not merely remain compliant.

They continuously regenerate their capability to sustain trustworthy operation.

Assurance Vitality™ is the long-term health and regenerative capacity of an assurance ecosystem to preserve, strengthen, and recover trustworthy operation.
