# Assurance Terminology™

---

## Purpose

Assurance Engineering™ establishes a common vocabulary for describing Operational Trust, assurance activities, and governance across regulated environments.

The terminology in this chapter provides standardized definitions that will be used consistently throughout the Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK™).

---

## Assurance Engineering™

The engineering discipline concerned with continuously designing, measuring, validating, governing, and improving Operational Trust across regulated physical, digital, AI-enabled, and cyber-physical systems.

---

## Operational Trust™

The measurable confidence that an Assurance Object can perform its intended function safely, securely, effectively, and compliantly under its current operating conditions.

---

## Assurance Object™

Any physical, digital, AI-enabled, organizational, or human-controlled entity requiring continuous assurance.

---

## Assurance Twin™

A continuously updated digital representation of the assurance state of an Assurance Object.

---

## Continuous Assurance™

The continuous collection, validation, analysis, and interpretation of assurance evidence.

---

## Assurance Evidence™

Information demonstrating the current assurance state of an Assurance Object.

---

## Assurance Passport™

A digital identity containing the assurance profile of an Assurance Object.

---

## Assurance Intelligence™

The transformation of assurance evidence into explainable operational knowledge.

---

## Governance Vision™

The human interface that visualizes Operational Trust through dashboards, mobile devices, wearable devices, voice interfaces, and spatial computing.

---

## Operational Trust Score™

A measurable indicator representing the current assurance state of an Assurance Object.

---

## Trust Decay™

The gradual reduction of Operational Trust resulting from outdated, missing, or invalid assurance evidence.

---

## Assurance Lifecycle™

The sequence of stages through which an Assurance Object progresses from design through retirement while maintaining Operational Trust.

---

## Evidence-to-Trust Compiler™

The analytical process that converts validated evidence into explainable Operational Trust decisions.

---

## Platform A

The commercial implementation layer of Assurance Engineering.

---

## Platform B

The advanced research and innovation layer of Assurance Engineering.
