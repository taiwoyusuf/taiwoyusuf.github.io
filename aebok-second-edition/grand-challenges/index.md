## Why Grand Challenges?

Scientific disciplines advance by pursuing questions that remain unanswered.

The Grand Challenges of Assurance Engineering™ define the long-term research agenda of the discipline.

Rather than prescribing immediate solutions, they identify enduring scientific and engineering problems whose resolution would significantly advance trustworthy operation across critical systems.

---

# Grand Challenge 1

Can Operational Trust™ become a universally measurable engineering property?

---

# Grand Challenge 2

Can Operational Trust be predicted before operational degradation becomes visible?

---

# Grand Challenge 3

Can Assurance Instruments™ continuously observe invisible assurance phenomena in real operational environments?

---

# Grand Challenge 4

Can human and AI reasoning be combined to improve Decision Confidence™ without reducing human accountability?

---

# Grand Challenge 5

Can Operational Trust be represented as a dynamic field spanning interconnected assurance ecosystems?

---

# Grand Challenge 6

Can Assurance Engineering establish internationally accepted measurement principles for Operational Trust?

---

# Grand Challenge 7

Can organizations continuously explain every operational decision using traceable assurance evidence?

---

# Grand Challenge 8

Can future Assurance Operating Systems™ autonomously coordinate assurance while preserving human oversight?

---

# Grand Challenge 9

Can Assurance Observatories™ improve global resilience by identifying emerging assurance phenomena before major failures occur?

---

# Grand Challenge 10

Can Assurance Engineering provide a common scientific language for trustworthy operation across healthcare, manufacturing, aerospace, energy, transportation, finance, and future autonomous systems?

---

# Guiding Principle

The purpose of these Grand Challenges is not merely to solve today's governance problems.

Their purpose is to inspire decades of scientific discovery, engineering innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and responsible technological advancement.

# Future Evolution

The Grand Challenges of Assurance Engineering™ will continue to evolve as new technologies, operational environments, and societal needs emerge.
