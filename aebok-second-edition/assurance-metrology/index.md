## Why Assurance Metrology?

Engineering disciplines depend upon reliable measurement.

Operational Trust cannot become an engineering quantity unless it can be measured consistently, explained transparently, and reproduced independently.

Assurance Metrology™ establishes the principles by which assurance quantities are defined, measured, calibrated, interpreted, and compared.

---

# Definition

Assurance Metrology™ is the science of measuring Operational Trust and related assurance properties across regulated systems.

Its objective is to ensure that assurance measurements are meaningful, reproducible, explainable, and comparable.

---

# Core Principle

If Operational Trust cannot be measured consistently, it cannot become an engineering discipline.

Measurement is therefore foundational.
