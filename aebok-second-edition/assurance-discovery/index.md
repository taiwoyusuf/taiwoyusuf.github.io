## Why Assurance Discovery?

Engineering disciplines continuously evolve through discovery.

New materials are discovered.

New diseases are discovered.

New particles are discovered.

Likewise, Assurance Engineering evolves through the discovery of previously unknown assurance phenomena, behaviors, relationships, risks, and opportunities.

---

# Definition

Assurance Discovery™ is the systematic identification, validation, classification, and explanation of previously unknown assurance phenomena.

---

# Core Principle

Operational environments continuously produce new assurance knowledge.

The role of Assurance Engineering is to discover it before failure reveals it.

---

# Discovery Domains

Examples include:

- Emerging assurance risks
- Hidden dependencies

- Novel AI behaviors
- Previously unseen governance patterns
- Human-AI interaction phenomena
