# Why Assurance Memory?

Operational excellence depends not only on what an organization knows today, but on what it remembers tomorrow.

Failures forgotten are often repeated.

Successful assurance practices that cannot be transferred are eventually lost.

Assurance Memory Theory™ studies how assurance knowledge is preserved, transferred, refined, and reused across operational, organizational, and generational boundaries.

---

# Definition

Assurance Memory™ is the structured preservation, retrieval, interpretation, and application of assurance knowledge over time.

---

# Core Principle

Operational Trust™ improves when organizations remember evidence, decisions, assumptions, outcomes, and lessons learned.
