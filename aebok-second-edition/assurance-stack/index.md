# Assurance Stack™

# Chapter 9

## The Reference Architecture of Assurance Engineering™

---

## Why the Assurance Stack Exists

Every engineering discipline has a reference architecture.

Computer Networking has the OSI Model.

Manufacturing has ISA-95.

Software has layered architectures.

Assurance Engineering requires a universal reference architecture that explains how Operational Trust is continuously established, maintained, and communicated.

The Assurance Stack™ provides that architecture.

# Why this Architecture Matters

Every Assurance Product implements one or more layers of the Assurance Stack.

Every research project extends one or more layers.

Every PhD contribution strengthens one or more layers.

Every future technology—including AI agents, robotics, wearable devices, and spatial computing—integrates into the Assurance Stack without changing its core architecture.

The Assurance Stack™ serves as the universal reference architecture for Assurance Engineering, providing a common language through which industry, academia, regulators, and technology providers can design, evaluate, and continuously improve trustworthy regulated systems.
