# Assurance Operations™

# Chapter 38

---

## Why Assurance Operations?

Engineering disciplines are defined not only by what they study but by what engineers do.

Assurance Engineering establishes a common set of engineering operations that continuously create, preserve, measure, communicate, and improve Operational Trust.

---

# Definition

Assurance Operations™ are the fundamental engineering actions performed upon Assurance Objects, Assurance Evidence, Assurance Twins™, and Operational Trust throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Core Principle

Every Assurance Engineering activity is composed of one or more Assurance Operations.

These operations form the engineering language of the discipline.

---

# Core Assurance Operations™

## Observe™

Acquire assurance-relevant observations.

Examples:

- Sensors
- Human observation
- AI observation
- System monitoring

---

## Verify™

Confirm evidence accuracy.

Examples:

- Validation
- Qualification
- Calibration
- Independent review

---

## Correlate™

Relate evidence across multiple Assurance Objects.

Examples:

- Equipment
- AI
- ServiceNow
- Cybersecurity
- Environmental monitoring

---

## Compute™

Determine Operational Trust and related assurance properties.

Examples:

- Trust
- Integrity
- Readiness
- Decision Confidence

---

## Explain™

Provide understandable justification.

Every assurance conclusion should be explainable.

---

## Predict™

Estimate future assurance behavior.

Examples:

- Trust Drift™

- Recovery

- Inspection readiness

---

## Preserve™

Maintain Operational Trust across change.

---

## Recover™

Restore degraded Operational Trust.

---

## Learn™

Improve future assurance capability.

---

## Evolve™

Continuously improve the assurance ecosystem.
