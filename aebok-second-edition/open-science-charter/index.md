# Why an Open Science Charter?

Scientific disciplines advance through transparency, collaboration, constructive criticism, and reproducible knowledge.

The Open Science Charter establishes the principles that govern how Assurance Engineering™ grows as an international scientific discipline.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Engineering Open Science Charter™ defines the principles that guide the responsible creation, evaluation, sharing, preservation, and improvement of assurance knowledge.

---

# Principle 1

Scientific ideas should be open to examination.

No concept is beyond criticism.

---

# Principle 2

Evidence outweighs authority.

Claims should be supported by reproducible evidence rather than reputation.

---

# Principle 3

Negative results matter.

Experiments that disprove a hypothesis contribute valuable scientific knowledge.

---

# Principle 4

Definitions should evolve carefully.

Changes should improve clarity while preserving historical traceability.

---

# Principle 5

Independent replication strengthens confidence.

Concepts become stronger when validated by independent researchers across different operational environments.

---

# Principle 6

Interdisciplinary collaboration accelerates discovery.

Assurance Engineering welcomes contributions from engineering, medicine, systems science, computer science, human factors, psychology, regulatory science, complexity science, and related disciplines.

---

# Principle 7

Knowledge should remain accessible.

Educational resources should encourage learning, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
