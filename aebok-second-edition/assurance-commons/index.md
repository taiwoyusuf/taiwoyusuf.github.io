## Why Assurance Commons?

Scientific disciplines advance through shared knowledge, shared methods, shared terminology, and shared collaboration.

No single organization owns mathematics.

No single university owns physics.

Likewise, Assurance Engineering should encourage a shared body of scientific knowledge while allowing organizations to build proprietary implementations.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Commons™ is the shared scientific foundation of Assurance Engineering consisting of open concepts, reference models, experimental methods, benchmark datasets, taxonomies, and educational resources that enable collaborative advancement of the discipline.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge grows faster when foundational science is shared.

Innovation grows faster when implementations compete.

The Assurance Commons™ separates scientific foundations from commercial products.
