## Why an Impact Framework?

Scientific disciplines should be evaluated not only by the number of publications they produce, but also by the value they create for society.

The Assurance Engineering Impact Framework™ provides a structured approach for evaluating how the discipline advances scientific knowledge, engineering practice, education, industry, and society.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Engineering Impact Framework™ is a multidimensional model for assessing the scientific, engineering, educational, operational, and societal impact of Assurance Engineering.

---

# Core Principle

The success of a discipline is measured by the improvements it enables rather than the terminology it creates.
