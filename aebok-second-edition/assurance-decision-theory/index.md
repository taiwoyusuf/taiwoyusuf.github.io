## Why Assurance Decision Theory?

Organizations rarely fail because information is unavailable.

They fail because decisions are made using incomplete, outdated, conflicting, or misunderstood assurance evidence.

Assurance Decision Theory™ studies how trustworthy operational decisions are produced.

Rather than asking:

**"What should the decision be?"**

Assurance Engineering asks:

**"Can this decision itself be trusted?"**

---

# Definition

Assurance Decision Theory™ is the study of how humans, AI systems, and organizations transform assurance evidence into trustworthy operational decisions.

# Assurance Decision Components

Every decision contains:

- Decision Context
- Evidence
- Operational Trust
- Risk
- Human Accountability
- Regulatory Constraints
- AI Recommendations
- Decision Confidence
- Decision Explanation
- Decision Outcome

---

# Decision Confidence™

Every assurance decision possesses a measurable confidence.

Confidence depends upon:

- Evidence completeness
- Evidence quality
- Trust computation
- Human review
- AI explainability
- Regulatory certainty
- Historical consistency

A decision should never be presented without its confidence level.

---

# Explainable Decisions™

Every operational decision must answer:

Why?

What evidence?

What assumptions?

Who approved?

Which regulations apply?

What risks remain?

---

# Decision Memory™

Every assurance decision becomes part of the organizational assurance memory.

Future decisions should learn from previous decisions.

Organizations improve by remembering.

---

# Human-AI Collaboration

The objective is not autonomous decision-making.

The objective is trustworthy collaborative decision-making.

AI assists.

Humans remain accountable.

---

# Decision Readiness™

Before execution, every operational decision should satisfy:

✓ Sufficient evidence

✓ Acceptable Operational Trust

✓ Regulatory compliance

✓ Human accountability

✓ Explainability

Only then is a decision considered Decision Ready™.
