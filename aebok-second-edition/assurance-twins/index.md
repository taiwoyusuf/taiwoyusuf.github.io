# Assurance Twins™

---

## 4.1 Introduction

Assurance Twins™ are one of the foundational innovations of Assurance Engineering.

While Digital Twins simulate how an object behaves, an Assurance Twin continuously represents how trustworthy that object is based on current operational evidence.

An Assurance Twin exists to answer one question:

**Can this Assurance Object be trusted right now?**

---

## 4.2 Definition

An Assurance Twin™ is a continuously updated digital representation of the assurance state of an Assurance Object throughout its lifecycle.

---

## 4.3 Difference Between Digital Twins and Assurance Twins

Digital Twins answer:

- How does the object perform?

Assurance Twins answer:

- Can the object currently be trusted?

Digital Twins simulate operations.

Assurance Twins evaluate trust.

Both can coexist.

---

## 4.4 Core Components

Every Assurance Twin includes:

* Identity
* Operational Trust Score
* Validation Status
* Cybersecurity Status
* Change History
* Risk Status
* Evidence Status
* Human Accountability
* AI Assurance Status
* Audit Readiness

---

## 4.5 Continuous Synchronization

Assurance Twins continuously synchronize with operational evidence including:

* Validation activities
* ServiceNow
* CMDB
* Cybersecurity tools
* AI governance systems
* Audit evidence
* Manufacturing systems
* Clinical systems

---

## 4.6 Trust Computation

Operational Trust is continuously recalculated whenever evidence changes.

Examples include:

* Patch installed
* Access revoked
* Validation completed
* AI model retrained
* Backup failure
* CAPA opened

---
