## Why Assurance Professions?

As Assurance Engineering matures, it will require professionals with specialized knowledge, responsibilities, competencies, and ethical obligations.

Different operational environments require different forms of assurance expertise.

Assurance Professions™ defines the emerging professional landscape of the discipline.

---

# Definition

Assurance Professions™ describes the specialized roles responsible for designing, measuring, maintaining, improving, and communicating Operational Trust.

---
