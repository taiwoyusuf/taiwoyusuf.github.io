## Why Assurance Field Theory?

Operational Trust does not exist only within individual systems.

It exists across relationships, environments, organizations, and time.

Assurance Field Theory™ provides a unified framework for understanding how assurance properties emerge across interconnected operational ecosystems.

---

# Definition

An Assurance Field™ is the distributed operational environment within which Assurance Objects continuously influence, strengthen, weaken, and reshape Operational Trust.

Rather than existing as isolated measurements, assurance properties emerge from interactions across the entire field.

---

# Core Principle

Operational Trust is not merely a property of an individual object.

It is an emergent property of the Assurance Field™ surrounding that object.

---

# Components of an Assurance Field™

Examples include:

- Assurance Objects™
- Assurance Twins™
- Evidence
- Human Decisions
- AI Decisions
- Regulations
- Cybersecurity
- Validation
- Suppliers
- Operational Context
- Time
- Organizational Learning

Together they create the Assurance Field.

---

# Field Dynamics

The Assurance Field™ continuously changes through:

- evidence updates
- regulatory changes
- AI learning
- human actions
- operational events
- environmental conditions

The field is therefore dynamic.

---

# Field Disturbances™

Events such as:

- cyber attacks
- failed validation
- supplier disruption
- AI model drift
- missing evidence

create disturbances within the Assurance Field™.

These disturbances influence Operational Trust beyond the immediate object.

---

# Field Stabilization™

Healthy assurance ecosystems continuously stabilize the Assurance Field™ through:

- governance
- accountability
- validation
- continuous monitoring
- learning
- resilience
