# Part VI — Evidence Intelligence™

# Chapter 6

# Evidence Intelligence™

---

## 6.1 Introduction

Evidence Intelligence™ transforms operational evidence into actionable assurance knowledge.

Rather than collecting evidence solely for audits, Evidence Intelligence continuously analyzes evidence to support Operational Trust, decision-making, inspection readiness, and governance.

---

## 6.2 Definition

Evidence Intelligence™ is the discipline of collecting, validating, correlating, interpreting, and continuously analyzing assurance evidence to support trustworthy operational decisions.

---

## 6.3 Why Evidence Intelligence Matters

Organizations generate enormous amounts of evidence every day.

Examples include:

* Audit records
* Validation evidence
* Change records
* Backup reports
* Cybersecurity logs
* AI model records
* Manufacturing events
* Clinical trial activities
* CAPAs
* Deviations

Raw evidence alone has limited value.

Evidence becomes useful only when transformed into explainable operational knowledge.

---

## 6.4 Evidence Lifecycle

Evidence progresses through:

1. Generation
2. Collection
3. Validation
4. Classification
5. Correlation
6. Trust Evaluation
7. Decision Support
8. Long-term Retention

---

## 6.5 Evidence Categories

Examples include:

* Governance Evidence
* Validation Evidence
* Operational Evidence
* Cybersecurity Evidence
* AI Evidence
* Clinical Evidence
* Manufacturing Evidence
* Human Accountability Evidence

---

## 6.6 Evidence Quality

Evidence should be:

* Accurate
* Complete
* Traceable
* Timely
* Explainable
* Verifiable
* Reproducible

---

## 6.7 Evidence-to-Trust Compiler™

The Evidence-to-Trust Compiler™ converts validated evidence into Operational Trust decisions.

Evidence → Rules → Trust Computation → Operational Trust → Decision

---

## 6.8 Relationship to Assurance Twins™

Evidence Intelligence continuously supplies Assurance Twins with current evidence.

Without Evidence Intelligence, Assurance Twins lose accuracy and reliability.
