# Part V — Continuous Assurance™

# Chapter 5

# Continuous Assurance™

---

## 5.1 Introduction

Continuous Assurance™ is the operational engine of Assurance Engineering.

Rather than evaluating trust only during periodic audits or inspections, Continuous Assurance continuously monitors assurance evidence and updates Operational Trust as conditions change.

---

## 5.2 Definition

Continuous Assurance™ is the ongoing collection, verification, interpretation, and evaluation of assurance evidence throughout the lifecycle of an Assurance Object.

---

## 5.3 Why Continuous Assurance Matters

Modern regulated environments change continuously.

Examples include:

* Software updates
* AI model changes
* Configuration changes
* Access modifications
* Supplier updates
* Manufacturing events
* Clinical trial activities
* Cybersecurity threats

Periodic reviews alone cannot accurately represent current Operational Trust.

---

## 5.4 Continuous Assurance Lifecycle

Continuous Assurance consists of:

1. Evidence Collection
2. Evidence Validation
3. Trust Computation
4. Risk Evaluation
5. Decision Support
6. Human Review
7. Continuous Monitoring

---

## 5.5 Evidence Sources

Continuous Assurance may collect evidence from:

* CMDB
* ServiceNow
* Manufacturing Systems
* Laboratory Systems
* Clinical Trial Platforms
* AI Governance Platforms
* Cybersecurity Platforms
* Validation Documentation
* Backup Systems
* Audit Records

---

## 5.6 Trigger Events

Continuous Assurance recalculates Operational Trust whenever events occur.

Examples include:

* Change Control Approved
* CAPA Opened
* Backup Failure
* Patch Installed
* User Access Modified
* Validation Completed
* AI Model Updated
* Risk Assessment Changed

---

## 5.7 Relationship to Assurance Twins™

Continuous Assurance continuously updates Assurance Twins with the latest evidence.

Without Continuous Assurance, Assurance Twins become static snapshots rather than living assurance representations.

---

## 5.8 Relationship to Governance Vision™

Governance Vision™ displays Continuous Assurance in real time through dashboards, wearable devices, voice interfaces, augmented reality, and future spatial computing environments.

---

## 5.9 Relationship to Platform A

Platform A operationalizes Continuous Assurance through:

* CITrust™

* AuditTrust™
