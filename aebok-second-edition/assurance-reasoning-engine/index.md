# Chapter 15

# Why an Assurance Reasoning Engine?

Operational Trust alone is insufficient.

Knowing that trust is 82% does not tell an organization what action should be taken.

The Assurance Reasoning Engine™ transforms assurance knowledge into explainable operational recommendations.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Reasoning Engine™ is the decision-support layer of Assurance Engineering.

It reasons over Assurance Twins™, Assurance Graphs™, Operational Trust™, Assurance States™, Assurance Genomes™, and evidence to recommend appropriate assurance actions.

---

# Inputs

The engine evaluates:

- Operational Trust
- Assurance State
- Assurance Genome
- Evidence Intelligence
- Continuous Assurance
- Assurance Graph
- Human Context
- Regulatory Context
- Organizational Policy
- Risk Appetite

---

# Outputs

Rather than simply reporting trust, the engine recommends actions.

Examples include:

- Continue operation
- Increase monitoring
- Request human review
- Initiate CAPA
- Perform validation
- Suspend operation
- Notify regulator
- Escalate to executive review
- Recompute Operational Trust
- Delay AI execution

---

# Explainable Reasoning

Every recommendation includes:

- Supporting evidence
- Applied assurance rules
- Relevant regulations
- Confidence level
- Alternative options
- Human accountability

---

# Human-Centered Reasoning

The Assurance Reasoning Engine™ assists decision-making.

It does not replace accountable human judgment.

---

# Relationship to Assurance Operating System™

The Assurance Operating System™ orchestrates assurance services.
