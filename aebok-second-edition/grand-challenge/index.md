# Why a Grand Challenge?

Scientific disciplines are often defined by the enduring problems they seek to solve.

The Grand Challenge of Assurance Engineering™ provides a unifying vision that guides research, engineering, education, and international collaboration over multiple generations.

---

# Definition

The Grand Challenge of Assurance Engineering™ is to enable humanity to responsibly understand, evaluate, and improve the operational condition of increasingly complex socio-technical systems before consequential decisions are made.

---

# Core Principle

As technological complexity grows, humanity's ability to understand and responsibly govern that complexity must grow at least as rapidly.

---
