# Why Assurance Ontology?

Engineering disciplines require a common vocabulary describing the entities, relationships, properties, and behaviors within their domain.

Without a shared ontology, different researchers, software systems, organizations, and AI agents may interpret the same concepts differently.

Assurance Ontology™ establishes the semantic foundation of Assurance Engineering.

---

# Definition

Assurance Ontology™ is the formal representation of the concepts, entities, relationships, properties, events, and constraints that define Assurance Engineering.
