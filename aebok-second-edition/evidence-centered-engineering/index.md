# Why Evidence-Centered Engineering?

Engineering decisions increasingly influence safety, quality, healthcare, manufacturing, artificial intelligence, and critical infrastructure.

As operational complexity grows, engineering decisions should be supported by structured evidence rather than assumptions, isolated experience, or fragmented information.

Evidence-Centered Engineering™ provides a disciplined approach for designing, operating, and improving systems through continuously justified engineering decisions.

---

# Definition

Evidence-Centered Engineering™ is an engineering paradigm in which significant operational decisions are explicitly supported, explained, and continuously refined using structured evidence.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering decisions should be proportionate to the quality, relevance, and transparency of the available evidence.

---

# Engineering Questions

Evidence-Centered Engineering™ asks:

- What evidence supports this decision?
- How trustworthy is the evidence?
- What evidence is missing?
- What assumptions are being made?
- What uncertainty remains?
- What additional observations would improve confidence?
