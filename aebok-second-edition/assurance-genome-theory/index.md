## Why Assurance Genome Theory?

Every Assurance Object™ possesses observable and measurable assurance characteristics.

Although Assurance Objects differ greatly, they share common assurance structures that determine how they behave, evolve, and contribute to Operational Trust.

Assurance Genome Theory™ provides a common engineering framework for describing those characteristics.

---

# Definition

An Assurance Genome™ is the structured set of fundamental assurance characteristics that define the behavior, capabilities, limitations, and trust properties of an Assurance Object™.

---

# Core Principle

Objects differ physically.

Their Assurance Genome™ describes how they behave within an assurance ecosystem.
