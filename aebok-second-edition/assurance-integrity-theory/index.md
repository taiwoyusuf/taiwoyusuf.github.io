## Why Assurance Integrity?

Operational Trust does not emerge spontaneously.

It emerges because the underlying assurance ecosystem possesses integrity.

Assurance Integrity™ represents the coherence, authenticity, traceability, accountability, and consistency of the assurance system itself.

---

# Definition

Assurance Integrity™ is the degree to which an assurance ecosystem maintains complete, authentic, explainable, traceable, accountable, and internally consistent assurance information throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Core Principle

Operational Trust is an observable consequence.

Assurance Integrity is one of its enabling conditions.

Organizations strengthen Operational Trust by preserving Assurance Integrity.
