## Why an Assurance Laboratory?

Scientific disciplines mature through experimentation.

Assurance Engineering requires environments where Operational Trust can be observed, simulated, challenged, measured, and improved before deployment into real operational systems.

The Assurance Laboratory™ provides this environment.

---

# Definition

An Assurance Laboratory™ is a controlled physical, digital, or hybrid environment used to study, validate, simulate, and improve Operational Trust.
