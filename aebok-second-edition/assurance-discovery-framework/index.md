# Why an Assurance Discovery Framework?

Engineering disciplines advance through disciplined discovery.

Meaningful discoveries are not accidental—they arise from systematic observation, rigorous experimentation, careful analysis, and continuous refinement.

The Assurance Discovery Framework™ provides a structured methodology for identifying, validating, and integrating new knowledge into Assurance Engineering.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Discovery Framework™ is the scientific process through which new assurance knowledge is discovered, evaluated, validated, and incorporated into the evolving body of knowledge.

---

# Core Principle

Every discovery should improve humanity's ability to understand, evaluate, or improve trustworthy operation.
