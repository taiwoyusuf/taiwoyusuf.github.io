## Why the Assurance Principle?

Throughout history, engineering has enabled humanity to construct systems of increasing complexity.

As complexity grows, direct human understanding becomes increasingly difficult.

Assurance Engineering exists to ensure that increasing complexity does not reduce humanity's ability to operate systems responsibly, safely, and confidently.

---

# Definition

The Assurance Principle™ states:

> As system complexity increases, assurance capability must increase at least proportionally to preserve trustworthy operation.

---

# Core Principle

Complexity without assurance eventually exceeds human understanding.

When assurance grows alongside complexity, trustworthy operation remains possible.

# Engineering Interpretation

Engineering progress should never be measured solely by capability.

It should also be measured by the ability to understand, explain, monitor, and responsibly govern that capability.

---

# Human Interpretation

The Assurance Principle™ protects human judgment.

As machines become more capable, humans require stronger assurance rather than greater control complexity.
