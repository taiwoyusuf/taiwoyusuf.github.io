## Why Trustworthy Environment Engineering?

Operational Trust does not emerge from individual technologies alone.

It emerges from the interaction of people, equipment, AI, governance, infrastructure, evidence, and operational context.

Assurance Engineering therefore designs trustworthy environments rather than isolated trustworthy systems.

---

# Definition

Trustworthy Environment Engineering™ is the discipline of designing, operating, measuring, and continuously improving environments in which trustworthy operation naturally emerges.

---

# Core Principle

Trustworthy operation is an environmental property.

No single component can guarantee it.

It emerges through coordinated interaction.
