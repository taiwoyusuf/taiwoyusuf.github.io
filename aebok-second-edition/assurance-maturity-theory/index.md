## Why Assurance Maturity Theory?

Organizations do not become trustworthy overnight.

Operational Trust develops through repeated learning, evidence accumulation, governance improvement, and organizational adaptation.

Assurance Maturity Theory™ explains how Operational Trust capability evolves.

---

# Definition

Assurance Maturity Theory™ studies the progressive development of an organization's capability to establish, maintain, explain, predict, and improve Operational Trust.

Unlike traditional maturity models that classify organizations into static levels, Assurance Maturity Theory™ examines the continuous evolution of assurance capability.

---

Operational Trust matures.

Human judgment matures.

The objective is continuous assurance capability growth.
