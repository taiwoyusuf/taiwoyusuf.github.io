## Why Assurance Potential?

Operational Trust measures the present.

Organizations must also understand their future capability to establish, preserve, recover, and improve Operational Trust.

Assurance Potential™ represents this future capability.

---

# Definition

Assurance Potential™ is the inherent capability of an assurance ecosystem to achieve, sustain, recover, and improve Operational Trust under changing operational conditions.

---

# Core Principle

Operational Trust describes the present.

Assurance Potential describes the future.

---

# Components of Assurance Potential

Examples include:

- Organizational Learning
- Human Expertise
- AI Capability
- Instrumentation
- Evidence Quality
- Governance Maturity
- Adaptability
- Resilience
- Innovation Capacity
- Knowledge Preservation

---

# High Assurance Potential

Organizations with high Assurance Potential:

- recover faster
- adapt better
- learn continuously
- improve Operational Trust
- absorb disruption
- adopt innovation safely

---

# Low Assurance Potential

Organizations with low Assurance Potential may appear trustworthy today but struggle to maintain trust when conditions change.
