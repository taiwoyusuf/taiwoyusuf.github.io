## Why Predictive Assurance?

Most operational systems detect problems after they become visible.

Assurance Engineering seeks to anticipate changes in Operational Trust before operational failure, regulatory deviation, or unsafe decision-making occurs.

Predictive Assurance™ transforms assurance from reactive observation into proactive engineering.

---

# Definition

Predictive Assurance™ is the science of forecasting future Operational Trust using evidence, historical behavior, organizational context, assurance dynamics, and intelligent reasoning.

---

# Core Principle

The objective is not predicting failure.

The objective is predicting declining trust before failure emerges.

---

Predictive Assurance™ estimates:

- Operational Trust trajectory

- Assurance Debt growth
- Trust Recovery time
- Inspection readiness

- Organizational resilience

# Human Oversight

Predictions inform decisions.

They do not replace human accountability.

Every prediction should include:

- confidence
- assumptions
- uncertainty
- evidence basis
