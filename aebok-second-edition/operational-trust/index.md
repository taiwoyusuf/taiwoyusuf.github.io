# Operational Trust™

---

## 2.1 Introduction

Operational Trust™ is the central scientific construct of Assurance Engineering.

Traditional governance determines whether an organization is compliant.

Operational Trust determines whether an Assurance Object can safely, securely, effectively, and compliantly perform its intended function at this moment.

It is not a certificate.

It is not an audit.

It is a continuously measurable engineering property.

---

## 2.2 Definition

Operational Trust™ is the measurable confidence that an Assurance Object can perform its intended function under its current operational conditions based on continuously validated evidence.

---

## 2.3 Why Operational Trust Matters

Modern regulated systems continuously change.

Examples include:

* AI model updates
* Software patches
* Access changes
* Configuration changes
* Cybersecurity threats
* Equipment maintenance
* Validation activities
* Environmental changes

A system may remain compliant on paper while no longer being operationally trustworthy.

Operational Trust closes this gap.

---

## 2.4 Operational Trust Equation

Operational Trust is influenced by multiple evidence domains including:

* Validation
* Cybersecurity
* Data Integrity
* Access Governance
* Change Control
* Backup Readiness
* Operational Monitoring
* Human Accountability
* Supplier Assurance
* AI Assurance

These domains collectively determine current trust.

---

## 2.5 Operational Trust Lifecycle

Operational Trust continuously changes through:

1. Design
2. Qualification
3. Validation
4. Operation
5. Maintenance
6. Change
7. Incident
8. Retirement

Trust is continuously recalculated throughout this lifecycle.

---

## 2.6 Trust States

Operational Trust may exist in different states.

Examples include:

* Trusted
* Trusted with Conditions
* At Risk
* Suspended
* Untrusted
* Unknown

These states provide actionable operational guidance.

---

## 2.7 Trust Decay

Trust naturally decreases over time whenever assurance evidence becomes stale.

Examples include:

* Expired access review
* Missed backup verification
* Delayed calibration
* AI model drift
* Missing cybersecurity patch
* Expired validation evidence

Trust must therefore be continuously refreshed.

---

## 2.8 Relationship to Assurance Twins™

An Assurance Twin continuously measures Operational Trust.

It provides a live representation of the trust state of an Assurance Object using current operational evidence.
