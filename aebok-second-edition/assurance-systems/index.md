## Why Assurance Systems?

Traditional engineering disciplines design systems that perform work.

Assurance Engineering designs systems that continuously establish, preserve, measure, communicate, and improve Operational Trust.

An Assurance System™ is therefore not defined by its technology but by its purpose.

---

# Definition

An Assurance System™ is an engineered socio-technical system whose primary purpose is to continuously generate trustworthy operational decisions through evidence, observation, reasoning, and accountability.

---

# Core Principle

Traditional systems answer:

"Can this task be performed?"

Assurance Systems™ answer:

"Can this task be trusted?"
