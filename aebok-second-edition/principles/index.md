# Assurance Engineering Principles

---

## Principle 1

### Evidence Before Assertion

Operational Trust must always be supported by verifiable evidence.

Trust is demonstrated—not assumed.

---

## Principle 2

### Continuous Assurance

Assurance is continuous rather than periodic.

Operational Trust changes whenever operational conditions change.

---

## Principle 3

### Trust is Computable

Operational Trust should be measurable using explainable evidence rather than subjective opinion.

---

## Principle 4

### Every Assurance Object Has a Trust State

Every regulated object possesses a continuously changing assurance state.

---

## Principle 5

### Trust Decays

Operational Trust naturally decreases whenever assurance evidence becomes outdated, incomplete, or invalid.

---

## Principle 6

### Human Accountability Remains Visible

AI may recommend.

Humans remain accountable.

---

## Principle 7

### Explainability

Every trust decision must be explainable.

Organizations should always understand why Operational Trust increased or decreased.

---

## Principle 8

### Assurance by Design

Operational Trust should be designed into systems rather than added after implementation.

---

## Principle 9

### Context Matters

Operational Trust depends upon intended use, risk, and operating context.

No universal trust score exists.

---

## Principle 10

### Continuous Improvement

Assurance Engineering promotes continual improvement through evidence, learning, and operational feedback.
