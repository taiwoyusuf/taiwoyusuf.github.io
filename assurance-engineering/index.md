# Assurance Engineering™

### The Discipline of Continuous Operational Trust

**Assurance Engineering™** is a proposed interdisciplinary field focused on designing, measuring, validating, governing, and continuously maintaining trust in regulated digital, physical, and AI-enabled systems.

The central question of Assurance Engineering is:

> **Can this operation, system, AI agent, evidence chain, or decision be trusted right now?**

---

## Why Assurance Engineering Is Needed

Modern regulated industries are moving toward artificial intelligence, autonomous agents, digital twins, intelligent manufacturing, wearable computing, and cyber-physical operations.

Traditional governance often asks whether a system is compliant, validated, or documented.

Assurance Engineering asks a deeper operational question:

> **Can trust be continuously demonstrated through evidence?**

---

## Core Concepts

### Continuous Assurance™

The ongoing collection, verification, monitoring, and interpretation of assurance evidence across the lifecycle of systems, processes, AI models, equipment, and decisions.

### Assurance Object™

Any entity requiring assurance, including systems, equipment, AI models, agents, datasets, suppliers, users, changes, CAPAs, clinical trial signals, manufacturing operations, and regulated decisions.

### Assurance Twin™

A continuously updated digital representation of the assurance state of an Assurance Object.

An Assurance Twin may include:

- Governance evidence
- Validation status
- Cybersecurity posture
- Data integrity status
- Access control state
- Change history
- Risk posture
- Human accountability
- Operational Trust Score™

### Operational Trust™

A measurable state indicating whether an Assurance Object can safely, securely, compliantly, and effectively perform its intended function at a specific point in time.

### Governance Passport™

The identity, ownership, accountability, lifecycle, evidence, and approval record of an Assurance Object.

### Evidence Chain™

The traceable path connecting control requirements, operational actions, evidence generation, review, approval, and audit readiness.

### Decision Ledger™

A verifiable record of significant operational and AI-assisted decisions, including supporting evidence and accountable decision makers.

### Trust Drift™

The measurable change in Operational Trust over time as systems evolve, evidence ages, risks emerge, or operating conditions change.

### Governance Vision™

The wearable and ambient interface for visualizing Operational Trust through smart glasses, mobile devices, voice assistants, augmented reality, and future spatial computing systems.
