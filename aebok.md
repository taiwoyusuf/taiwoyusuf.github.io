# Assurance Engineering Body of Knowledge (AEBOK) v1.0

## Core Thesis

Assurance Engineering is an interdisciplinary framework for continuously designing, measuring, validating, and maintaining Operational Trust in AI-enabled and regulated systems.

Its purpose is to answer one question:

**Can this system, equipment, AI agent, clinical signal, manufacturing operation, or quality decision be trusted right now, and what evidence proves it?**

---

## Core Novel Contributions

### 1. Operational Trust Twin

A live digital representation of the trust state of a regulated object, such as equipment, AI model, system, batch, clinical trial signal, or manufacturing operation.

### 2. Evidence-to-Trust Compiler

A logic layer that converts raw governance evidence into explainable trust decisions.

**Evidence -> Controls -> Assurance Rules -> Trust Score -> Decision**

### 3. Trust Decay Model

A model showing how trust decreases over time unless assurance evidence is refreshed.

Examples:

- Backup evidence decays after the expected review period.
- Access trust decays after review expiry.
- AI trust decays after model drift, retraining, or uncontrolled change.
- Validation trust decays after major system change.

### 4. Assurance Passport

A digital trust identity for every regulated object.

Passport types:

- CI Passport
- Equipment Passport
- AI Passport
- Agent Passport
- Trial Signal Passport
- Validation Passport
- Batch Passport

### 5. Governance Vision

A wearable and ambient interface that allows users to see Operational Trust in real time using smart glasses, mobile devices, voice, AR, or spatial computing.
